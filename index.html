Questions, answered

Is this equity or debt?
Debt. A secured, interest-only promissory note — not a fund interest. An equity participation option is available on request.
Where does my security sit?
A personal guarantee from the sponsor. In the distribution waterfall, notes are repaid after senior debt.
When do I get paid?
Interest is distributed quarterly in cash. Principal returns at maturity from unit sale proceeds.
What is the minimum?
$50,000. The full raise is $700,000.
Can I invest through an IRA?
Yes — self-directed IRA and 401(k) custodians are accommodated. Note it on the interest form.
What if units sell slower than modeled?
The term runs 18–24 months to absorb absorption risk, and launch pricing sits below every closed comp in the submarket.
