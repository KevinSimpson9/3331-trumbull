FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

Anything else goes straight to Todd.

Where is Trumbull North?

3331 Trumbull St, Detroit, MI 48208, in North Corktown, on the block where Trumbull meets Sycamore and Ash.

How many homes are for sale?

Phase 1 has 12 new construction townhomes for sale, in the two buildings facing Trumbull. The three back buildings are a future release.

What is the floor plan?

The Type A residence: 3 bedrooms and 2 baths (one with a double vanity, one single) across three levels, about 1,535 square feet.

Where do I park?

Residents park in the private parking court inside the block.

What is the NEZ-NC abatement?

Trumbull North is NEZ-NC Property Tax Abatement Eligible. The NEZ-NC Property Tax Abatement is available for 15 years on qualifying homes. Todd can walk you through how it applies to you.

When can I move in?

Permitting was completed in June 2026. Construction is planned for 2026–27, with Phase 1 move-ins planned for 2027–28.

How much are the homes?

Pricing is in the full package. Submit the Reserve form and it comes straight to you.

Are these homes for sale or for rent?

For sale only. All 12 Phase 1 homes are new construction townhomes for sale, each with a private rooftop terrace.

Are these condos or townhomes?

Townhomes. Each Type A home is a three-level townhome with its own front door and private rooftop terrace. Nothing is shared above or below you.

Moving to Detroit? How close are the homes to Michigan Central?

Michigan Central is about 1.2 miles from Trumbull North, and downtown is about 1.5 miles. For buyers moving to Detroit for work, these are new construction homes for sale near Michigan Central, Newlab and the Central Business District (CBD). Distances are estimates.

Is NEZ-NC the same as the NEZ Homestead program?

No. NEZ-NC applies to qualifying new construction homes like Trumbull North. The NEZ Homestead program is separate and covers existing homes. Todd can confirm how NEZ-NC applies to your purchase.
