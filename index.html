The Vision

A full city block in North Corktown.

Five buildings master-planned by 4545 Architecture around a private parking court, commanding 248 feet of frontage where Trumbull meets Sycamore and Ash — 25 for-sale townhomes plus a commercial space, 26 units in all. The design is permitted, the comps are closed, and the pricing is underwritten — the only thing left is to break ground.

One block from Sycamore Park (Bondy, sold out) and a short walk to Michigan Central and Corktown's restaurant row. Detroit's NEZ tax framework applies to comparable new construction in the district.
